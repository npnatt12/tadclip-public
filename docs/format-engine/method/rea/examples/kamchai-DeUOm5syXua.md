## 0. At a glance
A Thai "free tool you can run at home" explainer, 36.1 s. It opens on a split screen with a promise headline over
product footage, then alternates full-speaker blocks (big punch words) with split blocks (footage on top, a one-line
caption on the seam), and ends on a comment-keyword offer. Three things are worth learning: **(1)** the promise
headline at frame 0 states the benefit, not the product; **(2)** the speaker's shot is cut about every 1.5 s (30/min
in the body), with the layout switching every 4–9 s; **(3)** the CTA trades something concrete (a guide) for a
comment.

## 7. Why it holds attention (reasons, not outcomes)
1. **The promise is on screen at frame 0 and contains a concrete benefit** [S9]. Measured.
2. **Frequent cuts during continuous speech.** A speaker cut every ~1.5 s, with no pauses at all. Within-scene cuts
   are often not noticed when attention is on the content [S19], so the density reads as energy, not as
   interruption. Measured.
3. **The layout switches every 4–9 s** (2.5 → 8.7 → 17.4 → 21.4 → 28.7 s), marking each part (context, benefits,
   contrast, steps, recap). Event boundaries organise attention [S20]. Measured times, looked kinds.
4. **Short keywords instead of full captions in full-speaker blocks.** One phrase on the chest, key word in yellow:
   signalling [S3]. Full captions appear only where the eyes are on the footage (splits). Measured + looked.
5. **The face is never covered.** Seam captions sit above the head in splits, punch words sit on the chest [S2].
   Measured.

## 10. Your brief (example: a 40 s Thai explainer about a tool or service you offer)
- **Your angle:** <one line: what the viewer gets, for free or cheaply, and where>
- **Beat plan (40 s, scaled):**
| beat | job | your seconds | device | what you need |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | promise | 0–2.8 | split + promise headline (one accent phrase) | 1–2 striking shots of the result |
| 2 | context | 2.8–9.7 | full speaker + punch words | — |
| 3 | proof | 9.7–19.2 | split, 3 clips, seam captions | footage of the thing working |

| 7 | offer | 36.5–40 | comment keyword + handle | something you will actually send |
- **Rhythm targets:** body shot cuts 26–38/min (take boundaries at almost every sentence start, plus `zoom.snaps`);
  layout blocks 2.5–9 s; pauses removed. Do not cut words to hit the rate.
- **Hook:** `promise-headline` at 0 s over your best footage. Line shapes: "<result> at home, for <price>" · "How I
  <result> without <cost>" · "<number> minutes to <result>".
- **Make it yours:** your own accent colour and label style, a different seam treatment (for example a plate in your
  brand colour), and one device he doesn't use (a before/after, or a step counter).
- **Levers:** `broll mode: split` (`h` 960, `shift` 560), `impact look: headline` (y 890 for the hook, 1190 on the
  chest), `captions` `mini` + `onlyInSplits` + `strip`, `impact look: stair` `place: top`, `cta`. Thai limits: ≤ 17
  chars/s, ≤ 35 chars/line, breaks at phrase ends.
