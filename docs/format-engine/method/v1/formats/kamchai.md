# kamchai: split-screen Thai explainer (format `kamchai-split-explainer`, from 2 of 3 reels)

A fast Thai explainer about a product or tool, built on a split screen. Product or illustrative B-roll fills the top
half and the seated speaker (mic in shot, bookshelf behind) sits in the bottom half, which is why `face.top` reads
45–56 % and most text counts as `above`. The hook opens split. A 2–3 row Thai title in heavy white type with one
phrase in yellow (`#F8E838`) sits on the bottom edge of the B-roll just above the seam (y 46–47, `line_h` 6–7), and the
product's name or wordmark is on the footage. The body alternates every 2.5–8.6 s. Split blocks carry one-line Thai
captions on a translucent dark strip exactly on the seam (y 49.5–50.3, `line_h` ≈ 4.3, 1–1.5 s each; Vision misses the
strip). Full-speaker blocks drop the captions for one big punch phrase on the chest (y 60–65, `line_h` 5.4–9.8, keyword
in yellow) and sometimes a summary label above the head in black on a yellow box (`#FAF441`, y 23–24). Cuts are
very frequent: 36–46/min, median shot 1.2–1.4 s, p90 under 2.8 s, made of speaker jump cuts, layout switches and
B-roll changes in roughly equal parts. The voice has no measurable pauses. One reel ends on a comment-keyword CTA with
the Instagram handle. The third reel (DeQr_7OKgcZ, no face, a phone filming a monitor under a fixed POV title) is a
different series and is excluded. For a new clip, the fit hinges on whether there is footage for half the reel. The
pace should come from snaps and B-roll changes, never from cutting words.
